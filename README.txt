Before you deploy
  1. Find and replace  https://YOUR-DOMAIN  with your real site address (6 places in index.html, no trailing slash).
  2. Add your resume as assets/Ayusman_Nanda_Resume.pdf (the page links to it).
  3. Screenshots: save images in assets/shots/, then uncomment the SCREENSHOT SLOT blocks in index.html and set the src.
  4. Fill the YOUR STORY SLOT in the Background section with your own words.
  5. After deploying, test the link in LinkedIn Post Inspector (previews are cached).
